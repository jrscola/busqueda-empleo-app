Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026. This policy covers Busqueda Empleo, a private tool used only by its owner on the owner's own Gmail account.

What it accesses

  • Read access to Gmail, to find LinkedIn job alert emails and extract job titles and links, and to find replies from companies the owner has applied to.
  • Compose access to Gmail, to create drafts. The tool does not send email: the owner sends each draft by hand.
  • Modify access to Gmail, only to mark automatic application confirmations as read.

Storage and sharing

Extracted job titles and links, and the text of replies from companies, are stored on the owner's own computer. Access keys are stored in the macOS Keychain. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not given to third parties. The owner may review job titles and replies with an AI assistant of their choice.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Local data can be deleted by the owner at any time.
